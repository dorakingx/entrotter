## Unsent invitation

> We're testing Entrotter, an open-source local tool for comparing and replaying
> onchain agent decisions. Could we observe a 25-minute attempt to reproduce a
> small public scenario? We want to learn where it fails or adds no value. There
> is no sales commitment, and you can decline recording or quotation. We can use
> synthetic local data throughout.

Choose developers who currently build or review EVM transaction policies. Prefer
three independent people rather than treating colleagues in one internal test as
three separate customer organizations. State any prior relationship or incentive.
Do not offer compensation or spend money without additional approval.

## Session sequence

1. Obtain consent for notes and any recording. Ask about the participant's most
   recent simulation/review task, tooling, handoff and a specific failure. Do not
   present the pricing anchor yet.
2. Record OS, Python, Docker/Foundry prerequisites and exact source commits. Let
   the participant follow the supplied setup. Time installation separately; record
   help, errors and abandoned steps. Never silently preconfigure the environment
   and count that as cold onboarding.
3. Ask the participant to run the local example and explain the baseline/candidate
   difference, artifact hash and assumptions without coaching. Record task outcome
   and misunderstandings, including confusion about profit or historical replay.
4. Give them a colleague's recorded local result and ask them to reproduce it.
   Change one initial condition and observe whether the rejection is understood.
   A contributor can choose a public case of their own; never request real keys.
5. Ask what this replaced, what still requires bespoke scripts, what they would do
   next and what would make them abandon it. Only then discuss a possible paid
   team workflow and the $99/month hypothesis. Capture the actual answer, not a
   leading yes/no approval of the product.
6. Ask permission for any attributed quotation or public scenario. Record follow-up
   interest accurately; willingness to try again is not payment or a signed pilot.
